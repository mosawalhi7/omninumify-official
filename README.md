**OmniNumify** is an enterprise-grade, multi-jurisdiction calculation platform designed to model, compute, and simplify complex statutory, financial, and legal computations. Built with a scalable modular architecture, OmniNumify continuously expands to cover diverse regulatory frameworks, tax systems, and financial standards across global jurisdictions.

---

## 🌟 Key Highlights & Engineering Architecture

- **Zero Parametric Trust**: Every calculation strictly adheres to official primary statutory sources, executive regulations, and ministerial decrees rather than generalized approximations.
- **100% Client-Side Privacy**: All financial inputs and calculations execute locally within the client browser. No sensitive financial or personal data is collected, logged, or transmitted to external servers.
- **Pure Functional Core**: All calculation engines are architected as zero-side-effect pure functions, ensuring deterministic, mathematically verified outputs.
- **Modular Multi-Jurisdiction Engine**: Engineered for limitless scalability, allowing rapid onboarding of new countries, legal frameworks, and domain-specific calculation models.
- **Instant Speed & Modern Web Standards**: Powered by Next.js Server-Side Static Generation (SSG), TypeScript, and Tailwind CSS for instant edge delivery and responsive performance on all devices.

---

## 📊 Core Calculation Domains

OmniNumify provides comprehensive calculation suites across multiple specialized verticals:

- **Employment & Labor Law**: End-of-service gratuities, severance compensation, overtime pay, leaves settlement, and wrongful termination indemnities.
- **Taxation & Fiscal Brackets**: Progressive personal income taxes, corporate income taxes, withholding taxes, and Value-Added Tax (VAT/GST).
- **Social Security & Pensions**: Contributory pension schemes, early retirement annuities, unemployment insurance, and disability compensations.
- **Customs & Trade Tariffs**: Vehicle customs clearance tariffs, postal e-commerce import duties, and excise taxes.
- **Real Estate & Property**: Property transfer fees, real estate transaction taxes (RETT), municipal fees, and rental contract valuations.
- **Corporate & Commercial**: Company incorporation costs, judicial litigation fees, banking debt service ratios (DSR), and statutory allocations.
